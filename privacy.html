Private local integration

Privacy policy

This policy describes how the Operator handles information through a private integration run locally to access QuickBooks® Online.

Operator: Brown Donkatsu

Privacy contact: roy@browndonkatsu.ca

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Information the integration handles

The information depends on the permissions granted and the operations the Operator runs. It may include:

  • QuickBooks Online company information, identifiers, and business records returned by authorized requests. Those records may contain personal information about a company's customers, suppliers, employees, or other people.
  • Authorization information and access or refresh tokens used to connect to the authorized company.
  • Information the Operator enters into or generates with the integration.

How information is used

The Operator uses this information to authorize and operate the integration, make the QuickBooks Online requests the Operator configures, and maintain or troubleshoot the local setup. The integration is for private business use. The Operator does not intend to use QuickBooks Online information for advertising.

Where information is processed

The integration is intended to process and store information on the Operator's local computer. It sends the information needed to authorize access and make API requests to Intuit. The exact files kept on the computer depend on the local configuration.

This description assumes a local-only setup. If the Operator configures a tunnel, cloud backup or sync, hosted callback, analytics, logging, or another service that can receive personal or QuickBooks Online information, that service may process the information under its own terms. The Operator should identify that service and update this policy before using it.

If this policy is hosted on a website, the hosting provider may process technical information about visits to the page under its own terms. That website hosting is separate from the local integration.

Disclosure

The integration exchanges information with Intuit as needed for authorization and the QuickBooks Online requests the Operator makes. The Operator does not intend to sell this information or disclose it to other parties, except when the Operator configures another service as described above or when disclosure is required by law.

Storage, retention, and deletion

The Operator controls local storage and decides how long locally held information is kept. This policy does not promise a fixed retention period because it depends on the local setup.

To ask about information held by the Operator or request its deletion, contact roy@browndonkatsu.ca. Revoking the integration's authorization through Intuit may not remove copies already saved on the local computer. Deleting local copies may not revoke the authorization held by Intuit.

Security

Information on the local computer is subject to the security settings and practices used for that computer and its backups. The Operator should limit computer access and protect locally stored tokens and records. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Privacy rights vary by location. Contact the Operator at roy@browndonkatsu.ca with a privacy question or request. The Operator will handle requests as required by applicable law.

Changes and contact

The Operator may update this policy if the integration or its data handling changes. The current version will show its effective date above. For privacy questions, contact the Operator at roy@browndonkatsu.ca.
